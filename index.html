ABOUT THE COURSE

Learn the why behind safer care.

— ❧ —

This practical continuing-education course helps massage therapists integrate trauma-informed principles into everyday clinical practice. Participants examine how trauma, violence, adverse experiences, chronic stress, pain, medical experiences, and nervous-system responses may influence therapeutic touch, communication, body awareness, trust, consent, boundaries, and treatment participation.

MEET YOUR INSTRUCTOR - Crystal Gartman, RMT

Experience meets compassionate education.

— ❧ —

Crystal Gartman is a Registered Massage Therapist and Clinical Supervisor with 16+ years of hands-on clinical experience. She brings extensive expertise in therapeutic and sports massage, deep tissue and trigger point techniques, injury-focused care, and clinical assessment.

A graduate of the 2,200-hour Massage Therapy Diploma program at MH Vicars School of Massage Therapy, Crystal has worked extensively with elite and professional athletes in MMA, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, wrestling, hockey, and triathlon.

As a Clinical Supervisor at the Alberta College of Massage Therapy (ACMT), she helps students develop practical hands-on skills, clinical reasoning, and confidence.

Crystal is also the developer and instructor of Trauma-Informed Massage Therapy, combining her clinical experience with specialized training to teach therapists how to create safe, ethical, and client-centered treatment experiences.

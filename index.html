ABOUT THE COURSE

Learn the why behind safer care.

— ❧ —

This two-day, 10 CE hour course helps massage therapists bring trauma-informed principles into everyday practice. You’ll learn how trauma affects the body and nervous system, and how to create safety, choice and empowerment for every client.

THE COURSE

What you will learn

— ❧ —
  • How trauma impacts the body and nervous system
  • Strategies to create safety, trust and choice
  • Trauma- and violence-informed care for all clients
  • Effective communication, consent and boundaries
  • Professional self-care and nervous system regulation
  • Documentation that reflects trauma-informed care
  • Staying within scope: we are massage therapists, not trauma therapists
